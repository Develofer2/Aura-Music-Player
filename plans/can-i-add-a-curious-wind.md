## Full description
**AURA, your personal sound space.**

AURA is a local-first music player for the music you already own. Add your songs or whole folders and they are saved on your device, so your library stays with you, works offline and is never uploaded anywhere.

**What you can do**
- **Import in seconds:** drag in files or folders, or paste a direct link to an audio file you have rights to.
- **Listening canvas:** turn playback into a show with Artwork, Vinyl, Holo CD, Waveform, Oscilloscope, Aurora, Radio dial, Orbit and more.
- **Make it yours:** choose from many full-app themes in Appearance, or switch quickly from the top bar.
- **Playlists and favorites:** build mixes for any moment and keep your best tracks close.
- **Smart mixes from your own library:** pick a mood or purpose and see exactly why each song was chosen. No algorithm, no tracking.
- **Sound Studio:** equaliser, presets, reverb and stereo width.
- **Real listening stats:** minutes played and top tracks, calculated on your device.
- **Works offline:** install it as an app and keep listening without a connection.
- **Backup:** export your library details so playlists and favorites are never lost.

**Privacy first.** No sign-up, no ads, no cloud. Your music and history stay in your browser.
